Biology Lecture - Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy.

The process takes place in chloroplasts, which contain chlorophyll.

Plants absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and water through their roots.

Light energy is used to produce glucose, and oxygen is released as a byproduct.

Photosynthesis consists of two main stages: the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle.
